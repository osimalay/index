What would you like to know?

Here are some helpful answers to get you started.

What is METRO LOAN?

METRO LOAN is an informational platform designed to help visitors explore loan categories, understand borrowing considerations and make informed financial decisions.

Does METRO LOAN give out loans directly?

No. METRO LOAN does not itself approve or disburse loans. Any loan application, offer or approval is handled by the relevant lender.

Can I compare different loan options?

You can explore different loan categories and learn about factors such as interest, fees, repayment periods and lender conditions.

How can I estimate my repayments?

Visit the Calculator page to explore illustrative repayment estimates. Actual loan terms depend on the lender and the agreement offered.

Does using the website guarantee loan approval?

No. Viewing information or using a calculator does not guarantee eligibility, an offer or approval.

What should I check before accepting a loan?

Review the lender, interest rate, fees, repayment schedule, penalties and total amount payable. Make sure the repayments fit your budget.

How can I contact METRO LOAN?

Visit the Contact page to prepare your enquiry. Message delivery will require a connected backend.
