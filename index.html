BEFORE YOU APPLY

Understand the things lenders may consider.

Eligibility requirements differ between lenders and financial products. Knowing what may be reviewed can help you prepare.

  • Identity and personal information
  • Regular income or a source of repayment
  • Existing financial commitments
  • Credit history, where applicable
  • Ability to meet the proposed repayment schedule
  • Any additional lender-specific requirements

BORROW RESPONSIBLY

A good financial decision starts with understanding.

Borrowing can be useful when it is carefully considered. Before taking a loan, take time to understand the full commitment.

01

Review the total cost

Look beyond the amount you receive. Understand interest, fees and the total amount repayable.

02

Check affordability

Consider your regular income, essential expenses and other financial responsibilities.

03

Understand the terms

Read the agreement carefully and ask questions about anything you do not understand.

04

Plan your repayments

Make sure the repayment schedule is realistic for your financial circumstances.

NEED MORE INFORMATION?

Frequently asked questions.

Here are answers to some common questions about loans and using METRO LOAN.

What is METRO LOAN?

METRO LOAN is a financial information website designed to help visitors explore loan options and understand borrowing considerations.

Can I compare different loan options?

You can explore different loan categories and review the information provided about their purposes, terms and considerations.

Does using a loan calculator guarantee approval?

No. A calculator provides estimates only. It does not represent a loan offer, approval or guarantee of eligibility.

What should I check before taking a loan?

Review the interest, fees, total repayment amount, repayment dates and any other conditions before agreeing to borrow.
