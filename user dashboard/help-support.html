Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some common questions.

How do I apply for a loan?

Open Apply for a Loan from your dashboard. Review the available information, complete the required fields and follow the application instructions.

Where can I check my loan application?

Visit My Loans to review the loan information and any application status details available on your account.

How can I check my repayment dates?

Select Repayment Schedule from your sidebar to review your repayment information.

How do I update my personal information?

Visit My Profile to review your personal account information and available profile options.

What should I do about an incorrect transaction?

Check your Transaction History and keep the relevant transaction details available when requesting assistance.

How can I protect my account?

Keep your password private, use a strong password and never share your login details or verification codes.
