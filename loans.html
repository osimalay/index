UNDERSTANDING LOAN OPTIONS

Different needs. Different financial solutions.

A loan is money borrowed from a lender with an agreement to repay it, usually with interest and possibly additional fees, over an agreed period.

The right borrowing option depends on several factors, including what you need the money for, how much you need, your income and your ability to make repayments.

At METRO LOAN, we make it easier to explore different loan categories and understand the considerations that may affect your decision.

KNOW WHAT MAY BE REQUIRED

Understanding loan eligibility.

Lenders use different eligibility criteria when reviewing loan applications. Requirements may depend on the type of loan, the lender and the applicant's financial circumstances.

Some common requirements may include:

  • Valid identification and personal details
  • Proof of income or a reliable repayment source
  • Bank statements or financial records
  • Employment or business information
  • Credit history, where applicable
  • Other documents requested by the lender

Meeting general requirements does not guarantee loan approval. The final decision belongs to the relevant lender.

LOAN INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions about loans.

Find answers to common questions to help you understand borrowing and compare your options.

What types of loans are available?

Common loan categories include personal, business, emergency, education, short-term and purchase financing. Availability depends on the relevant lender.

How do I choose the right loan?

Start by identifying your financial need. Then compare loan terms, interest, fees, repayment periods and affordability.

What determines whether I qualify?

Lenders may consider your income, identity, financial records, credit history and ability to repay. Requirements differ between lenders.

Does a lower monthly repayment always mean a cheaper loan?

Not necessarily. A longer repayment period may reduce individual instalments while increasing the total interest paid. Always compare the total repayment amount.

Can I repay a loan earlier than expected?

Some lenders allow early repayment, but conditions and possible charges vary. Check the loan agreement before making an early repayment.

Does using METRO LOAN guarantee approval?

No. METRO LOAN provides general loan information. Loan approval, offers and eligibility decisions are determined by the relevant lender.
