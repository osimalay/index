BEFORE YOU PROCEED

Important things to remember.

Taking time to check the details can help you avoid unnecessary financial pressure.

01

Check the lender

Verify the lender's identity, reputation and relevant authorisation before sharing information or accepting an offer.

02

Understand every charge

Ask about interest, service charges, processing fees, late payment penalties and the total repayment amount.

03

Protect your information

Use verified channels and be careful about sharing passwords, PINs, one-time codes or sensitive financial details.

04

Borrow within your means

Do not borrow more than you can reasonably repay after accounting for your regular expenses and commitments.
