Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use METRO LOAN.

Information you provide

If you complete a form, you may provide details such as your name, email address, phone number or message. Use only information you are comfortable sharing.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to your enquiry or support the requested website interaction. We do not use it to promise loan approval.

Third-party providers

Loan decisions, applications and agreements are handled by the relevant lender or provider. Review that provider’s privacy policy before submitting an application.

Questions
